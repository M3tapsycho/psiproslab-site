Sources

For anyone who wants to check the science.

Brain and Sleep

  • Melatonin: Ferracioli-Oda et al., 2013, meta-analysis, PLOS One
  • Sleep therapy (CBT-I): Trauer et al., 2015, meta-analysis, Annals of Internal Medicine
  • Creatine: Avgerinos et al., 2018, systematic review, Experimental Gerontology
  • L-theanine and coffee: Owen et al., 2008, trial, Nutritional Neuroscience
  • Omega-3 and mood: Liao et al., 2019, meta-analysis, Translational Psychiatry
  • Lion's mane: Mori et al., 2009, trial, Phytotherapy Research
  • Exercise and thinking: Smith et al., 2010, meta-analysis, Psychosomatic Medicine
  • Mediterranean diet: 2024 meta-analysis, GeroScience
  • Focus and task switching: Monsell, 2003, review, Trends in Cognitive Sciences

Building blocks

  • ABC model and CBT: David, Cristea and Hofmann, 2018, Frontiers in Psychiatry
  • Three brains and emotion takes over: Arnsten, 2009, review, Nature Reviews Neuroscience
  • Instinct is faster: LeDoux, 2000, review, Annual Review of Neuroscience
  • White bear effect: Wegner et al., 1987, experiment and Abramowitz et al., 2001, meta-analysis
  • Clogged engine: Wiehler et al., 2022, Current Biology and Inzlicht and Friese, 2019, review
  • Relief reward: Sirois and Pychyl, 2013 and Steel, 2007, meta-analysis, Psychological Bulletin
  • TIPP: Linehan, DBT Skills Training Manual
