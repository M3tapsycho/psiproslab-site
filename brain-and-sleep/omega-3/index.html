Omega-3B

Fish oil. A small help with low mood.

Your brain is partly built from these fats.

Good for

A small help with low mood. Little effect on memory.

How

Oily fish twice a week, or about 1 g of fish oil a day.

Watch out

If you take blood thinners, ask your doctor first.
