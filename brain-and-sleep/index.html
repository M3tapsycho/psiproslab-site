Brain & Sleep

What really helps

Many pills promise a better brain. Most do little. Ask your doctor before you take anything, especially if you take medicine, are pregnant or are ill.

Basics

They beat every pill.

SleepA

7–9 hours, at the same times every day. Get daylight in the morning, and dim screens at night.

MoveA

Walking, cycling or swimming helps your thinking and your mood.

EatB

Lots of vegetables, fish and olive oil, like in the Mediterranean.

FocusB

Do one thing at a time and take breaks.

L-theanineB
