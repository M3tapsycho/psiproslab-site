About

Pros De Zutter

Behavioral Psychologist (MSc, Maastricht University)

ΨProsLab turns behavioural science into practical tools: short protocols, apps and one-to-one sessions that help you build calm, focus and habits that last.

The approach is evidence-based and measurable: we start from what research shows works, set clear goals, and track whether things actually change for you.

Methods

  • Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) — working with thoughts and behaviour patterns that keep problems going.
  • Behaviour change & habits — small, structured steps that stick.
  • Breathing & HRV training — paced breathing and humming practice for calm and focus.
  • Motivational interviewing — finding and strengthening your own reasons to change.
