The white bear effect

Why "don't think of it" backfires.

Trying not to think about something keeps it in your head. Your brain can't do "don't"; it only hears the thing itself. Fighting the thought also costs energy. (Wegner's research, confirmed by a meta-analysis.)

Example: Try not to think of a white bear for one minute. It keeps popping up.
