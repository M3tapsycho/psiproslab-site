Emotion wins

Why you say things you regret in a fight.

When an emotion is very strong, it turns down your thinking brain. You act first and think later.

Example

In a heated fight with your partner, you say hurtful things you don't mean. When you calm down, your thinking brain comes back and you think, "Why did I say that?" Regret follows.
