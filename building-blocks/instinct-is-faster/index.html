Fast instinct

Why you jump before you know why.

Your survival reflex acts before you feel or think. Nothing is switched off; it simply wins the race.

Example

You see a garden hose in the grass and jump back. A moment later you feel fear. Only then does your thinking brain say, "It's just a hose." (LeDoux's research on the brain's fast survival circuit.)
