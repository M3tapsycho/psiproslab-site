Emotional bucket

Why small things overflow on a bad day.

Stress and strong feelings fill a bucket inside you over the day. When it's full, even small things make it overflow.

Example

After a stressful day, a spilled glass of milk makes you snap at your kids. On a calm morning, you'd just wipe it up.
