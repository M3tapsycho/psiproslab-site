TIPP: empty the bucket fast

Four fast ways to calm your body.

Four quick ways to calm your body when emotions run high. (A skill from Dialectical Behaviour Therapy.)

  1. Temperature: Cold water on your face for 30 seconds.
  2. Intense exercise: A few minutes of hard movement.
  3. Paced breathing: Breathe out longer than in, for example in for 4, out for 6.
  4. Paired muscle relaxation: Tense a muscle while breathing in, let go while breathing out.

Health check: ask your doctor first if you have a heart condition, take beta-blockers or have an eating disorder.

Example: Panic rises before a meeting. You splash cold water on your face, and your heart slows down.
