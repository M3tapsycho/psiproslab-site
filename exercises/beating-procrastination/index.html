Procrastination

Four steps from avoiding to starting.

Deal with the feeling first, then the task.

  1. Name the feeling: "This task makes me anxious" or "bored."
  2. Lower it: Use TIPP, or remind yourself the feeling will pass.
  3. Make the start tiny: Commit to only 5 minutes.
  4. Notice the good feeling of doing: Progress feels better than escape.

Health check: ask your doctor first if you have a heart condition, take beta-blockers or have an eating disorder.
